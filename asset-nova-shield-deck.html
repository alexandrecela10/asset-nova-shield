Kill criteria

Recall drops below 100% (missed a real threat), or precision <60% after 8 weeks of tuning, or the contracts team stops the daily review for 4 consecutive weeks: pause deployment, return to backtest mode. Every disposition (approve/edit/dismiss) feeds the self-improving loop — the agent gets sharper on your project without your data improving anyone else's.

Honest assessment

Four risks to address, with clear next steps

Each risk has an owner, a de-risking action, and a decision point.

$
Value Risk — Will customers pay for this?

The $30-80M value claim stacks two low-confidence assumptions. "Validated exposures" is soft — customers can bless big numbers costlessly. Contracts teams may fear the exposure register surfacing their own misses.

De-risk: 5-8 customer discovery interviews (owner PMO leads, contracts managers in MENA) to validate willingness to share archives, daily review ritual appetite, and what "value" means to them.

Next step: Customer interviews via Orbitron's existing relationships

⚙
Feasibility Risk — Can we build this reliably?

100% recall (never miss a real threat) across heterogeneous corpora (scanned Arabic + English, different contract forms). OCR pipeline, XER parsing complexity, per-contract-form deadline engine. Open-weights model performance on domain-specific legal language.

De-risk: Phase 0 backtest on a closed project answers this before any commercial commitment. The eval harness (built — see prototype) runs offline with zero dependencies.

Next step: Engineering feasibility review with CTO + ML lead

⚖
Viability Risk — Can we afford to build and sell it?

In-region sovereign GPU at scale. Cost per document at 500K docs/project. 9-18 month sales cycles in MENA. IKTVA localization, SACS-002 certification, tech E&O insurance for deadline liability. Team investment vs other NovaOS priorities.

De-risk: Bottoms-up cost model (GPU provisioning, team, certification timeline). Test EPC contractor wedge as faster revenue path if owner cycles stall.

Next step: Cost-to-build assessment with CTO and finance

✎
Usability Risk — Will the workflow actually stick?

The daily review ritual is new work on top of a contracts manager's day. High precision required or they tune out. Dispositions create a discoverable record (political risk). Governance of visibility (who sees what was flagged) is design-critical.

De-risk: Prototype-test the review ritual with 2-3 prospect contracts managers. Team dispositions (not individual). Triage volume cap. Design for their existing artifact (event register), not a new dashboard.

Next step: Design review with UX + prospect user testing

Appendix — The bigger picture

Shield can make the whole relationship healthier

Today, claims are adversarial by default. Disputes drag on for months, cost millions in legal fees, and damage relationships for both sides. Shield can change that — for owners first, and eventually for contractors too.

Today's reality

Disputes are slow, expensive, and lose-lose

  • Average ME dispute takes 12.5 months to resolve (Arcadis)
  • Contractors overstate claims by ~2x because evidence is weak and negotiations are long (IPA)
  • Owners reject claims they should partially accept because they can't find the proof
  • Both sides spend millions on external claims consultants assembling the same evidence separately
  • Relationships deteriorate — the next project starts with less trust
What Shield enables

Fast, precise, substantiated claims — on both sides

  • For owners (now): defend against inflated claims with evidence, not guesswork. Respond in days, not months
  • For contractors (Phase 2): build substantiated, well-evidenced claims faster. Claims that are part of the job, not a fight
  • When both sides have good evidence, disputes resolve faster and fairer
  • Less money spent on external consultants and arbitration
  • Better relationships. The next project starts with more trust, not less
⚖

The win-win: better evidence on both sides means faster, fairer outcomes for everyone

Start with the owner (the buyer with the clearest pain and the biggest budget). Once Shield proves the model works, offer a contractor variant. Same technology, same evidence standard. When claims are fast, precise, and substantiated, they stop being disputes and start being business as usual.
