Dismissals detail: When the team dismisses a false alarm, Shield raises its threshold for that pattern. Fewer irrelevant flags over time.

Edits detail: When the team rewrites a notice before sending, the edit is captured. Draft quality improves to match how your team actually writes.

Approvals detail: Confirmed events grow the set of known-good examples used to test every update. No change ships unless it passes on all confirmed events.

Sovereignty: All retraining happens inside the customer's own environment (in-region VPC or on-prem). Dispositions never leave. Your project data never improves someone else's agent. No shared model, no data export, no cross-tenant learning. The agent gets smarter on your projects and only your projects. Guaranteed via NovaOS.

Kill criteria

Recall drops below 100% (missed a real threat), or precision <60% after 8 weeks of tuning, or the contracts team stops the daily review for 4 consecutive weeks: pause deployment, return to backtest mode. Every disposition (approve/edit/dismiss) feeds the self-improving loop -- the agent gets sharper on your project without your data improving anyone else's.

Full AI Engineering success: 100% threat detection recall (never miss a real threat); maximize precision progressively. 99.5% citation accuracy (deterministic verifier). 0 hallucinations in approved notices. 100% deadline accuracy (rules engine). Less than $0.05/doc blended cost.

Appendix -- The bigger picture

Shield can make the whole relationship healthier

Today, claims are adversarial by default. Disputes drag on for months, cost millions in legal fees, and damage relationships for both sides. Shield can change that -- for owners first, and eventually for contractors too.

Today's reality

Disputes are slow, expensive, and lose-lose

  • Average ME dispute takes 12.5 months to resolve (Arcadis)
  • Contractors overstate claims by ~2x because evidence is weak and negotiations are long (IPA)
  • Owners reject claims they should partially accept because they can't find the proof
  • Both sides spend millions on external claims consultants assembling the same evidence separately
  • Relationships deteriorate -- the next project starts with less trust
What Shield enables

Fast, precise, substantiated claims -- on both sides

  • For owners (now): defend against inflated claims with evidence, not guesswork. Respond in days, not months
  • For contractors (Phase 2): build substantiated, well-evidenced claims faster. Claims that are part of the job, not a fight
  • When both sides have good evidence, disputes resolve faster and fairer
  • Less money spent on external consultants and arbitration
  • Better relationships. The next project starts with more trust, not less
⚖

The win-win: better evidence on both sides means faster, fairer outcomes for everyone

Start with the owner (the buyer with the clearest pain and the biggest budget). Once Shield proves the model works, offer a contractor variant. Same technology, same evidence standard. When claims are fast, precise, and substantiated, they stop being disputes and start being business as usual.
